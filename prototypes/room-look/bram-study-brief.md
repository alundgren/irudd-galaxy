# Bram study constraints

This is preparation for the conditional bust increment in the
[overnight brief](overnight-brief.md). It does not authorize starting before the
coordinator inspects the board proof. All visual decisions remain provisional.

## Existing identity

The current core identifies Bram Okafor as Lantern Dock's dockmaster. He keeps
the berth and lamps, welcomes the pilot and explains trade and station needs.
There is no dedicated being sheet. Use these existing sources:

- [Body recipe](../../game/modules/core/recipes/person_bram.toml): navy coat,
  dockmaster's cap and brass badge.
- [Head recipe](../../game/modules/core/recipes/head_bram.toml): brown skin,
  dark hair and navy cap. These colours describe identity; the old geometry is
  not an anatomy reference.
- [Current content](../../game/modules/core/start.toml): introduction and actions.

The selected [room and face concept](../../game/docs/visual-upgrade/references/room-and-face.png)
is the appearance target. Inspect the right-hand face study and the same person
in the left-hand room view. Do not use the rejected prototype heads as a starting
model or as a target.

## Visible priorities

The concept shows a mature adult face, strong cheek and brow volumes, a broad
nose, defined eyelids and lips, greying short curls and facial hair, and an
asymmetric, attentive expression. The coat has substantial fabric, a raised
mustard collar, teal panels and restrained coral trim. Treat these as visual
study choices, not newly established biography or a precise canonical age.

First establish a coherent head, neck and shoulders under simple light. Inspect
front, side and three-quarter views; camera perspective must not conceal weak
anatomy. Preserve the face's adult proportions and clear individual features at
conversation size. Add deliberate hair masses and clothing construction before
fine painted marks. Avoid repeated round pieces as the entire hairstyle.

The final lighting study should explain facial volume and resemble the concept's
warm illumination and violet fill. Keep a neutral diagnostic so the light cannot
conceal incorrect forms. Record materials and colour-management settings.

## Limited motion if appearance supports it

Keep an untouched static checkpoint. Demonstrate a blink and a brief glance or
small expression change using a coherent deformable face. Inspect eyelid contact,
eye direction, mouth corners, hair, contours and shadows throughout the movement.
This is an acting study, not a complete dialogue rig. Do not add voice or invent
new dialogue to make it look finished.

## Deliverable

Editable Blender source, front/side/three-quarter evidence at close and talk size,
normal-speed motion if attempted, standard export and isolated reimport. Clearly
separate authored features from unsupported export effects. Do not claim that a
Blender render proves game-runtime appearance or performance.
