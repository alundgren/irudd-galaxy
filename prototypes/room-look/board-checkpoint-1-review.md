# Board checkpoint 1: coordinator inspection

Inspected `blender-board-lab/renders/cp1_goal_comparison.png` and
`blender-board-lab/renders/cp1_reference_camera_neutral_diagnostic.png`.
This is a visual iteration checkpoint, not the independent final review.

The new asset uses the intended copper rail, cream plaque and grey-green panel.
It is closer in colour placement than the earlier scripted board. It still reads
as flat plates, with insufficient differences between front, bevel and side faces.
The target has bright narrow edges, dark undersides and inset fasteners that explain
its construction. Random dots in the current texture do not supply that depth.

Provisional coordinator scores: look adherence 2/5, graphical quality 2/5.
Character, liveness, motion, performance, navigation and interaction are not judged
by these static asset images. No owner score or acceptance is implied.

## Next correction

- Match the selected first plaque region before expanding the board. Present both
  the full context and the same bounded plaque/rail region. Missing neighbouring
  cards should not dominate the comparison or be counted as a material defect.
- Correct the corner bracket and upper block proportions. The bracket is too wide
  and flat; the upper block is a large front plate instead of a compact block with
  distinct side and lower faces. Recheck each landmark, not only mean camera error.
- Give the plaque rim, clip and rail convincing thickness and bevels. Add the
  target's narrow bright upper edges and dark lower faces through geometry and
  controlled lighting. Fasteners need inset dark surrounds and small bright rims.
- Tune the rail and clip towards ochre rather than saturated orange-red. The panel
  should transition from warm grey near the lamp to cooler grey-green away from it.
  Do not recolour the entire backing tan based on one illuminated sample.
- Reduce scattered speckles. Place wear at contact points and edges. Keep the
  plaque's broad cream field calm; improve the icon's internal light/dark marks
  only after construction reads correctly.
- Provide one oblique lit view with the next reference-camera render. This will
  distinguish a shallow asset from a camera/light problem. Keep a plain diagnostic.

Do not start Bram or expand into a complete board yet. The early GLB predates this
checkpoint and cannot establish current export fidelity.
