Irudd Galaxy · Known player-facing changes (Lantern Reach playtests)

What this is. This page lists behaviour to carry into the rebuild, gathered from playtests of the previous build. It is not a technical plan. It says what the player should experience, and the agent decides how to build it. Numbers in brackets are feedback item numbers from the playtest log.

Priority reflects how strongly Andreas felt about it: ▲ High ■ Medium ● Low. Items marked possibly still open were not rechecked in the latest playtest.

Already decided (do not contradict)

  • Station: the station up close. Docking frames the station large, each part is a clickable module with a sign naming its keeper, and a room opens beside its module while the station stays visible. A station key (one icon per part) gives one-click access, and each icon carries a news ribbon (New, Changed, Closed) and a one-line headline on hover.
  • Talk: short bubbles over whoever speaks, click to advance. The ship log is one small icon, and no text panel grows with history.
  • Input: mouse first. Every room is reachable by clicking, and the movement keys always fly the ship off the dock.
  • Agency: nothing changes the player's cargo, credits or fitted parts without a deliberate choice and a readable receipt.
  • Readability: text is pure black or pure white, never grey. State colours come from a colourblind-safe set and always pair with a shape, arrow or word. Disabled buttons are drawn differently (for example dashed), never as greyed text.
  • Calm: the game is solo and forgiving, and nothing punishes the player for bumping into things.

Keep: already right in the previous build (don't regress)

  • Deliveries are an explicit Deliver / Sell / Keep choice with a receipt, and cargo never appears or disappears silently (#21, #30).
  • Cargo carried for someone else is clearly theirs and can't be sold (#32).
  • New controls and gifts are offered, never fitted silently (#33).
  • NPCs speak in the first person (#39).
  • Directions in dialogue are vague but correct. The lantern current with "east of the Wharf" is the reference clue level (#29, #40).
  • Flying past a region's edge, a current carries you back (#47).
  • Nothing overlaps at 1280×720, credits use one format everywhere, and all active goals show with who gave them (#34, #35, #41, #46).
  • Talk doesn't linger on screen after undocking, and hints retire after a few uses (#19, #20).
  • Rock shading changes smoothly while mined, and the mining beam ends cleanly on the surface (#12).
