What landed

Phase 1Fixes and flight · eb9fc2c

  • Cargo agency (#21, #30): Kettle offers Deliver / Sell / Keep instead of taking the ice. Every hand-over shows a receipt ("Delivered 20 ice to Odo · +120 cr"). Ilse's plate is an offer you accept. The script host refuses changes to the hold, credits or fitted parts unless they cite a player reply.
  • Exploits (#31, #32): docking counters share the economy's recount rule, so tapping W and Space no longer farms dockings. Ilse's plate is marked as hers in the hold, the market won't buy it, and only that plate completes b07.
  • Gifts and copy (#33, #38–#40):
    • The lane chart is offered, not fitted silently.
    • NPC lines are in the first person.
    • News only comes from here or nearby.
    • Directions are correct, checked by a compass test.
  • Flight preset: tank controls (W/S thrust, A/D turn, Space brake, Shift boost ×2).
    • Inertia 0.40 and auto-brake to a stop in 1.0 s.
    • Top speed 165 u/s, turn 180°/s, zoom 0.80 = 550 u. The scale is 1 lab px = 0.55 u, agreed on the PR.
    • Speed, turn and zoom are data.
    • A Space press does what's in reach; holding it brakes. The hint names which.
  • World edge (#47), mining (#48), controls text (#42): past a region's edge a current carries you back, with lantern chevrons and a literal hint. Mining starts without a nudge, at the drawn rock's edge. The controls text reads its numbers from the rules.
  • Determinism (#42, 1.4): station-art checks and Luau math run on libm, with bit-pinned tests. ^ and math.noise are refused by the pack check.
  • Rendering (1.6): rocks morph smoothly while mined, band edges are anti-aliased, the hatching sticks to the rock, the chip colour fades in, and the beam ends on the surface.
  • Tooling (1.7): the adapter is logged at startup. An FPS counter is on F2 or galaxy play --fps.
  • HUD (#34, #35, #41, #43, #44, #46): nothing overlaps at 1280×720, all active goals show with who gave them, one credit format is used everywhere, and the window opens at 1280×720. Hints retire after a few uses, and the save remembers them.

Phase 2Talk and the ship log · d23047a

  • Talk bubbles: they rise from the speaker's part of the station, oldest first. Click or Enter for the next line. Choices are buttons, and keys 1–9 still work. Nothing stays on screen after you undock, and unsaid lines come back at the next docking.
  • The ship log (the Log icon, bottom left, or L):
    • Jobs, cargo you carry for someone, and open offers are pinned at the top.
    • There's one line per visit, with Replay.
    • Notices and receipts are separate tickets.
    • Older entries page.
  • Lines said in flight go to the log, and the icon shows "N new".

Phase 3The station up close and the hullwright · df10aea

  • The station: docking zooms into the station. Modules lift on hover with an orange ring, and a click glides to the room beside them. Esc, right-click or Back returns.
  • The station key: one icon per part, with New, Changed and Closed ribbons counted in dockings.
  • Parts as data: station parts are data (station_parts.toml) with a validator. The game master can close, build, reopen and remove parts.
  • Hullwright model:
    • Four sockets, plus a control rack whose modules stay fitted.
    • Power and mass budgets; extra mass lowers speed and turn.
    • A locker per hull.
    • Ready, Make and Friends sources.
    • Juno at Cinder Hook fits drill mounts only.
  • Cradle view: drag a part, or click it and then a socket.
    • Sockets that can take it get a dashed sky-blue ring.
    • A ghost part shows where it would go.
    • A before → after preview with ▲/▼.
    • An over-power fit is refused.
    • The crane fits the part with a skippable animation.
    • The Wren then wears the part, in the cradle and in flight.

Phase 4A station that lives, the live layer and logging · 7c28b10

  • Tilted docked view (40°), with life driven by the sim:
    • Crate piles follow stock.
    • The crane carries your sale.
    • A crowd gathers for new notices.
    • A lamplighter lights a lamp each station pulse.
    • Workers stand at parts that are being built.
    • Real NPC craft dock and leave.
    Faction themes are data (themes.toml). Q or the PAUSED card switches full or quiet life.
  • Happenings as data (#27): lamp night when Kettle's greenhouse reaches stage 2. At stage 3, Odo offers a crate of 4 rations, with Accept / Not now and a receipt.
  • World memory: summarised entries per station, person and faction in the save, each tagged with its source (authored, game master or live layer). It has an FTS5 index and an empty table kept for embeddings later. Search it with galaxy memory search.
  • Live world layer on OpenRouter's auto router (low cost tier, a session_id per station): banter, traffic, brags and small discounts.
    • The model only gets a closed list of ids. Everything is validated and clamped.
    • Results go into station history only.
    • It never blocks play, and simply skips when there's no key or a call fails.
  • LLM call log: <stem>.llm-log.sqlite beside the save, one row per call. It's pruned on startup into daily rollups. Query it with galaxy llm-log.

Phase 5Radio and generated music · 345bfad

  • Radio: every station and two lane beacons have a channel with a range.
    • The signal fades with distance, with a little static at the edges, and crossfades where channels overlap.
    • The first time a channel comes into range, the log says "New signal: Kettle Dock radio".
    • The widget is at the bottom right: Prev / Off / Next, or R.
    • It shows authored text bits, and live ones too when a key is set.
  • Generated music (Lyria 3 clip through OpenRouter):
    • Tracks are cached in <stem>.music/ beside the save and never generated again.
    • Caps: 2 per run, and $0.40 per save, kept in exact money and booked before each call.
    • An HTTP 402 or a refused key stops generation for the run.
    • The radio plays cached tracks, or quiet synthesised placeholder loops.

What didn't land, and why

  • Solid rock collision: with rocks 40–60 u apart it walled off mining spots and stalled the pilots. Ships still pass over rocks; mining stops at the drawn edge.
  • Hub quarters: quarter signs and two-click framing aren't built, because no slice station is big enough. Quarters are only a sort key in the station key.
  • Cradle extras:
    • The Wren isn't animated rising into the cradle; the camera glide stands in for it.
    • The drone bay exists only as a socket.
  • Living station extras: two banners during a dispute, module shadows, and the wharf working on NPC ships aren't built. Theme sounds are names only.
  • Discounts: the market room doesn't mark the discounted row itself. A notice, a ribbon, the keeper's line and the price show it.
  • Balance (#36, #37): left open on purpose, as the issue says.

Known issues

  • Platforms: only Windows 11 was checked, with an NVIDIA GPU on Vulkan. Linux and macOS are unchecked for tonight's platform-layer changes: GPU (morph and lights), audio (kira MP3 decoding, the radio track) and file paths (the call log and music cache beside the save).
  • Radio audio: nobody has listened to the radio mix and generated music on a real device while flying. Only the automated tests cover the mix.
  • Mouse drag in the cradle: not tried by hand in a real window. The same state machine is tested headless.
  • Live layer: some routed models refuse reasoning: none. Those calls are logged as failed and skipped.
  • Music costs: with a key set, entering a channel's range can buy a clip (about $0.04), up to 2 a run and $0.40 a save. A reported cost above the estimate could pass the save cap once, and then generation stops.
  • Small things:
    • At the very start of a new game, two NPC ship names near the Wharf can overlap for a moment.
    • At 960×540, holding a part leaves room for one tray row, and the tray pages.
    • The mind.toml comment on the music spend cap describes the older rule.
  • A test failed once under load: gm_examples failed once while four agents were compiling, and passes on its own and in every later full check.

The Lyria response shape

Found
RequestPOST /api/v1/chat/completions with model, messages, modalities: ["text","audio"], audio: {"format":"mp3"}, stream: true and session_id. Audio comes back only when the request streams.
ResponseServer-sent events with : OPENROUTER PROCESSING keep-alive lines.
  • The first delta.content was "<instrumental>".
  • One chunk carried all the audio as base64 in delta.audio.data (about 990,000 characters).
  • The last chunks carry finish_reason: "stop" and usage.
AudioMP3 with an ID3 header: 192 kbps, 44.1 kHz, stereo, 744,659 bytes, about 30.8 s. It took about 8 s to generate.
Costusage.cost: 0.04, all of it in cost_details.upstream_inference_completions_cost. 57 prompt tokens and 4 completion (audio) tokens. The actual cost matched the listed $0.04 per clip exactly. Provider: Google AI Studio.
