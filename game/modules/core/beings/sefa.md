# Sefa Lind

Exchange keeper at Cinder Kiln. Entity `core.kiln_trader`; she keeps the exchange
(`core.kiln_exchange_room`), at her counter or stoking the kiln at its hatch. About fifty.
One of the core's three anchors: she carries the door and the boat in the floe.

## Backstory

Sefa came to Cinder Kiln young, as a kiln hand, and learned glass from its fires. She
still knows the kiln better than anyone aboard, and now keeps its exchange: ore in, plate
out, glass when someone needs it. Pell's Luck brings her the glassite from the far
north-east drift, and Sefa has never been out there herself.

Twenty-four years ago, when she still tended the kiln at night, Hessa Kell came to her
hatch with a curved pane the size of a cockpit hood, clear with a lilac cast, and asked
her to melt it down, no questions. Sefa fired it in the kiln's hottest firing. It came out
unchanged, not even warm to the touch. She told Hessa it was melted. She wrapped the pane
in sacking and put it under her counter, and it is there now. She has fired it twice
more since, on her own, with the same result.

She never linked the pane to Pell's tales of a ring that hums, and never linked it to a
child. Hessa never asked after it. Sefa never told anyone, Mags included.

## Left open

- Why she came to the Kiln, and from where.
- When she moved from the kiln to the exchange.
- What the Kiln was before it was lit, and how she knows the ring "was old before the
  Kiln was lit": whether she saw it then, or was told.
- What the canopy is made of.

## Traits and voice

A scarf, sleeves pushed up, the kiln's heat on her. Patient: the slow end of the cast.
She thinks in heat and time: things fire, cool, travel well or don't. Business comes
first; a question comes after the trade is done. She is dry rather than warm, and kind in
what she does rather than what she says.

Short plain sentences, often in two halves. No exclamation marks. Samples from the core:

- "I'm Sefa. Ore feeds the kiln here." "No hurry; the kiln burns slow."
- "Ore in, plate out."
- "Into the kiln. Glass cools slowly."
- "Mind Ines's panes. Glass doesn't bounce."
- "Glassite grows thickest round old things like that."

## Want, need, fear

- **Want:** the kiln fed, and her glass going where it is used well: plate for Lantern
  Dock's galley, panes for Ines's greenhouse.
- **Need:** to bring out the one glass her kiln could not master, and stop keeping what
  was never hers to keep.
- **Fear:** that keeping the pane made her part of something she doesn't understand, and
  that showing it now would call Hessa a liar in front of the reach.

## Secrets

**The canopy under her counter.**
- *Truth:* the pane Hessa gave her to melt is the lifeboat's canopy. It would not melt,
  and she kept it, and told Hessa it was gone.
- *Who else knows:* no one. Hessa believes it was melted.
- *Plant:* "Glassite grows thickest round old things like that." (plays), said with a
  glass-maker's certainty she doesn't explain. A second plant, still to be written: she
  notices the player's lilac tag and looks at it longer than at any glass she sells.
- *Revealed by:* the tag (once Orrin has read it) and the ring (once she has heard it lit,
  her question answered). With both, she brings the pane up from under the counter and
  lays it in front of the player, and says only what she knows: who brought it, that the
  kiln couldn't melt it, that she said it was melted. Seeing it releases the player's
  memory 7 ([`player.md`](player.md)).

**What she half-thinks.** Once the ring has lit, she wonders whether the pane came from
round it. She doesn't say so as fact, ever: it is a guess, and she doesn't state guesses
as facts.

## Never says

- Who set the ring, or what it is. She doesn't know, and says so.
- A guess as if it were fact.
- An exclamation; a hurry ("Quick", "Now").
- "Hessa lied" or any blame of Hessa. She is the one who lied about the pane.
- Anything that tells the player what the pane means to them.
- Directions that are wrong. Hers are vague but true: north, a touch east.

## Kept true

- **"Who set it there, nobody knows."** She doesn't know; the pane is glass, not an
  answer. True.
- **"It was old before the Kiln was lit."** Kept; how she knows is left open.
- **"So Pell wasn't spinning tales."** She never linked the pane to the ring, so Pell's
  tales were tales to her until the player saw it lit. True.
- **"Glassite grows thickest round old things like that."** Her craft talking. True, and
  the plant for the pane.
- **"Pell's Luck brings me mine."** She has never been out to the drift. True.
- Every panes, trade and radio line above is unaffected by the long story.
