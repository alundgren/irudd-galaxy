# The player

The pilot of the small tug the game starts in. Entity `core.ship`. Twenty-seven years old.
One fixed origin, the same in every game; this sheet holds only their past.

## Backstory

**Through the door.** Twenty-four years ago the old stone ring by the glassite drift opened
for less than a minute. A small lifeboat came through it, carrying one child of about
three, asleep and alone. The boat ran west across the reach on its own drive and stopped
among the ice. A lilac glass tag hung on a cord round the child's wrist.

**On Cold Kettle.** Hessa Kell, six years into hauling ice, found the boat in the ice and
the child in it. She took the child aboard Cold Kettle, gave them a name and raised them on
her barge from about three to about six. To the few she traded with she said the child was
her cousin's, sent out from inward. She froze the empty boat into a floe out past the ice
camp, where no one cuts. She gave the boat's canopy to Sefa Lind, then a young hand at the
Kiln, to melt down. When Cold Kettle took on stores at Cinder Kiln, Hessa sometimes
brought the child into the office, and once Mags Ferrow lifted them up to see her chart.

**Inward.** At about six the child fell ill, and the reach had no doctor. Hessa put them
aboard a hospital ship bound inward. She told its crew only that the child was found in
the ice, gave no name of her own and logged nothing. The intake card read: "Child, about
six. From the reach, found in the ice. No kin recorded." The tag went with them.

**The yard.** They got well and were placed with a foster family on a yard station several
lanes inward. The family told them what the card said and nothing more, because they knew
nothing more. They grew up at the yard, learned to fly its tugs, and kept the tag on its
cord. They never cut stone in a reach.

**Back.** At twenty-seven they bought a tired tug, sold with a Lantern Dock registration,
so it wears Lantern Dock's lantern on its left wing. They flew it out to the reach the
card named and started where the ore is: the ore drift. That is where the game begins.
The tag hangs from the canopy frame on its cord.

## Left open

- The child's name. Hessa gave them one; the foster family may have kept it. Whoever
  writes Hessa's recognition decides whether it is ever said.
- Who sent the child, from where, and why alone. The far side of the door is open: write
  nothing that rules out going through it to somewhere wholly new.
- How the boat crossed the reach to the ice, and what its drive was.
- What the illness was.
- The foster family, the yard station and its name, and the years between six and
  twenty-seven, beyond the facts above.
- Which keepers besides Hessa, Mags and Sefa ever knew a child lived on Cold Kettle. Don't
  assume any did.
- What the character hoped to find in the reach. That is the human's.

## Traits and voice

Not written. In the core the player has no voice of their own: they choose answers on
talk buttons (`Accept`, `Not now`, `A stone ring that lit up`), and those answers stay
plain. How the pilot is, and how they feel about any of this, is the human's.

## Want, need, fear

Not written, on purpose. These are the human's.

## Secrets

The character remembers things the human doesn't know yet. Each memory is released by a
place, a person or an object, once, and goes into the log as plain lines. None is in the
core yet; the draft lines below are for whoever writes them in.

**The rule for every memory: facts and things seen, heard or smelled, never feelings.**
No "you feel", "you remember fondly", "something pulls at you", "you have to", no word
that judges anyone, and no line that points to where to go next. A memory never says what
it means, and never names the ring, the door or Hessa as the thing it is about. The human
connects them, or doesn't.

| # | Released by | What is remembered (draft log line) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **The reach**, on the first flight (place) | "The card said: found in the ice, out in the reach. This is the reach." |
| 2 | **Hessa's voice**, her hail on the radio (person) | "That voice once sang four lines while ice came up on a hoist. The tune, not the words." |
| 3 | **The tag held to the light** at the cradle, once Orrin points at it (object) | "A small chart glowing in front of a child's face, a way drawn on it, fading." |
| 4 | **Mags's office**, her chart on the wall (place) | "A red pencil. Being lifted up to a chart, and a finger on a circle with no name." |
| 5 | **The ring**, the first time it lights (place) | "A ring seen from inside, its groove lit all the way round. Then dark." |
| 6 | **Cold Kettle up close**, once a line is passed to her (object) | "A stove with a kettle on a chain. Ice melting in a pan beside it." |
| 7 | **Sefa's canopy**, once she shows it (object) | "Lilac light inside a small boat. A voice counting in words the character doesn't know, the same count again and again." |

After memory 1, memories 2 to 5 come in whatever order play brings them; 6 and 7 come
later. No memory releases the
whole story: together they say a child was out here, on a barge and in a boat, and
nothing about why. Hessa's recognition, the boat in the floe and the other door are not
memories; they are the long threads (`README.md`), found in the world.

## Never says

What the game never puts in the player's mouth, on a talk button or in the log:

- A feeling about the past: "I missed you", "I always knew", "I'm home".
- A judgement of Hessa, Mags or Sefa for what they did.
- A claim to know what the ring is, or what is past it.
- "I remember" followed by more than a memory's own facts.
- An answer that only fits one feeling. Where the player answers about the past, one of
  the answers is plain, so the human can pass without choosing a stance.

## Kept true

- **"Welcome, pilot."** (Bram) and **"New tug?"** (Hessa's hail): the pilot is new to the
  reach as a pilot, and Hessa doesn't know them. True.
- **The ore drift is where you start**, and the opening teaches cutting from scratch
  (Sefa's "Watch the stone under your beam."): the character flew yard tugs but never cut
  stone in a reach. True.
- **The tug wears Lantern Dock's lantern on its left wing** (README, "The look"): it was
  sold with a Lantern Dock registration. True.
- **The ring wakes once, when the ship comes within 40 units, and lights for no one
  else**: Pell's route keeps clear and Pell hears it hum without it lighting (Sefa:
  "Pell won't cut near your ring. She says it hums."). True.
- **Not yet reconciled, waiting on implementation:** the README makes the cutting lens
  the first part a player can buy and names off-screen stations in the bottom stack
  (`rules.bearings`). The new direction (`../../../docs/direction.md`, 2.2) makes the map
  the first earned upgrade and replaces direction hints with fog of war, a minimap and a
  map. The core changes when the map is built.
