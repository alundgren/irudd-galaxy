# Hessa Kell

Skipper of Cold Kettle, the ice barge that hauls Lantern Dock's water in from the ice camp
beyond the west drift. Entity `core.kettle_skipper`; she comes aboard a station only once
her barge is towed in (kept: Lantern Dock's greenhouse and Ines's table; mended: Mags's
office until she sails). Past sixty. One of the core's three anchors: she carries the
child in the ice and the boat in the floe.

## Backstory

Hessa has hauled ice on Cold Kettle for thirty years, from the ice camp out west to Lantern
Dock, alone or nearly. She knows the ice drift and the dark past it better than anyone.

Six years into the work, one night at the ice camp, she saw a flash far off north-east, by
the old stone. Mags raised her on the radio, asking. Hessa said she saw nothing. Days
later, among the ice, she found a small lifeboat that had come to a stop there, and in it
a child of about three, asleep and alone, with a lilac glass tag on a cord round their
wrist.

She took the child aboard Cold Kettle and gave them a name. To the few she traded with she
said the child was her cousin's, sent out from inward. She froze the empty boat into a
floe out past the ice camp, where no one cuts. Its canopy she couldn't break, so she took
it to the Kiln's night hand, a young Sefa, and asked her to melt it down, no questions.
Sefa told her it was melted.

For three years the child lived on her barge. When they were about six they fell ill, and
the reach had no doctor. Hessa put them aboard a hospital ship bound inward, told its crew
only that the child was found in the ice, gave no name of her own and logged nothing. She
left the tag on the child's wrist. She has never asked after them, so that nothing could
lead back: not to them, not to her.

She believed then, with nothing to go on, that a child who came out of a light in the old
stone would be taken from her by someone: whoever sent them, or whoever came asking. She
has never told anyone any of it.

## Left open

- The child's name. She remembers it. Whether she ever says it is for whoever writes her
  recognition.
- What she has seen out past the ice camp in thirty years.
- Where she came from before Cold Kettle, and the cousin she named.
- Whether she ever went back to the boat in the floe.
- What she would do if she knew the pilot was that child. Her recognition is a long
  thread; nothing here decides it.

## Traits and voice

A weathered face, grey hair under a teal knit cap, a heavy coat. Blunt and warm, quick to
thank and slow to complain. She'd sooner be working than talked about. Her barge is "my
Kettle" and "she". She sings while the ice comes up on the hoist, four lines, always the
same tune.

Short spoken sentences, often with the barge in them. "Bless you" is hers. Samples from
the core:

- "New tug? I haul Lantern's water in from the ice. If my lamps go dark out west, come
  looking."
- "A line! Bless you." "Your tow, your call."
- "I'm Hessa. Thirty years she and I hauled ice."
- "Her tanks for Noor's beds, then. I'd sooner have sailed her."
- "Earned. I'll sail when you do."

## Want, need, fear

- **Want:** to keep sailing her Kettle and hauling the ice. Kept at Lantern Dock, she
  makes her peace with minding the tanks; mended, she sails.
- **Need:** to learn what became of the child, and to own what she did: the finding, the
  lie and the sending away.
- **Fear:** that the child didn't live, and that asking would lead someone to them, or to
  her.

## Secrets

**The child.**
- *Truth:* she found the child in a boat in the ice, raised them three years and sent
  them inward unlogged.
- *Who else knows:* no one, as far as she knows. Mags knows the child was real and that
  "saw nothing" was a lie ([`mags.md`](mags.md)).
- *Plant:* her hail, "If my lamps go dark out west, come looking." (plays), and
  "Thirty years she and I hauled ice." (plays), which takes in the three years the child
  was aboard. Still to be written: her hauling song, heard once on her radio.
- *Revealed by:* her recognition, a long thread. It may come through the tag (she tied it
  on), the song, Mags's watch book or Sefa's canopy. Which one, and when, is left to that
  work.

**The boat in the floe.**
- *Truth:* the lifeboat is frozen into a floe out past the ice camp.
- *Who else knows:* no one.
- *Plant:* still to be written. Cold Kettle's route runs from the ice camp; she knows the
  ice past it.
- *Revealed by:* Hessa herself, after her recognition. A long thread.

**The canopy.** She believes Sefa melted it ([`sefa.md`](sefa.md)). When she learns it
wasn't is part of the boat-in-the-floe thread.

## Never says

- Where the boat came from, as fact. She doesn't know.
- "My child" or any claim on the pilot before her recognition.
- Anything that tells the pilot what to feel about her: "You must hate me", "Forgive me",
  "You're home now". Her own feelings she may say; the pilot's are theirs.
- A plea for the pilot to stay, or to choose her side.
- A complaint about the work. She'd sooner haul than talk.

## Kept true

- **"New tug?"** in her hail: she doesn't know the grown pilot. True.
- **"Thirty years I've hauled ice."** and **"Thirty years she and I hauled ice."**: the
  child came six years in and lived aboard for three of the thirty. True.
- **"If my lamps go dark out west, come looking."**: her lamps go dark in Cold Kettle's
  job, as the core plays it. True; read again later, it is also a plant.
- **Her tow story and both endings** (kept or mended, the fee taken or kept): unaffected.
  The sheet adds nothing that either ending contradicts.
- **The ice camp beyond the west drift** is her home mooring; the boat lies past it,
  outside the drift the player cuts, so no floe the player mines holds it. True to
  `start.toml`.
