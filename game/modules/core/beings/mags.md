# Mags Ferrow

Dockmaster at Cinder Kiln. Entity `core.kiln_master`; she keeps the office
(`core.kiln_office_room`), at her desk or marking the field chart on its board, with a
porthole that looks out on the reach. About fifty. One of the core's three anchors: she
carries the door and the child in the ice.

## Backstory

Mags has watched the field from the Kiln's office for most of her working life. She
keeps its charts and its berth log, and she names what she charts: drifts, buoys, the
lanes. Her oldest chart came with the office. The old stone ring north-east of the Kiln
is on it, drawn and unnamed.

Twenty-four years ago she had the office's night watch. Through the porthole she saw a
flash north-east, by the old stone, under a minute long. She wrote it in her own watch
book with the time and the bearing, and raised Cold Kettle on the radio, the only ship
out that night. Hessa said she saw nothing.

Some weeks later Hessa had a small child aboard, her cousin's, she said, sent out from
inward. Mags kept the berth log: no ship had come out from inward that season. She knew
then that "saw nothing" was a lie, and she said nothing. When Cold Kettle took on stores
at the Kiln, Hessa sometimes brought the child into the office, and once Mags lifted them
up to see her chart. Three years on, the child was gone, inward, and Mags asked nothing.

She never marked the flash on any chart, and never named the ring, so no pilot would go
looking. The watch book is in her desk.

## Left open

- Who kept the office before her, and who drew her oldest chart.
- Where she came from, and why she stays.
- Whether she ever told anyone what she saw. As written, she has not.
- What she thinks the flash was. She has never let herself guess on paper.

## Traits and voice

A brimmed hat, a red pencil behind her ear, the chart always within reach. Brisk and
exact: numbers, bearings, what the field holds. She keeps order and wants everyone home
by the end of the shift. Under it she is protective, and quick to take Hessa's side.

Short statements, facts first, rarely a question unless it's an offer. Samples from the
core:

- "You're at Cinder Kiln." "I'm Mags. I watch the field from here."
- "Each of us pays less as our stock fills, so spread your loads."
- "The field still holds 36 ore." "The field's worked out. Good shift."
- "Bram kept Cold Kettle for good. Hessa's grounded."
- "Your ring's on my oldest chart, unnamed."

## Want, need, fear

- **Want:** a field in order, its charts true, every pilot and barge home safe.
- **Need:** to make her own chart true: to put the flash where it belongs and say what
  she saw, even though it was Hessa's secret to keep.
- **Fear:** that her silence let a child be sent down the inward lanes without a trace,
  and that the truth told now will cost Hessa more than the lie ever did.

## Secrets

**The flash and the lie.**
- *Truth:* she saw the ring flash the night the child came, wrote it down, and knew Hessa
  lied. She has kept that for twenty-four years.
- *Who else knows:* no one. Hessa doesn't know Mags knows.
- *Plant:* "Your ring's on my oldest chart, unnamed." (plays). Unnamed on purpose. A
  second plant, also playing: while Cold Kettle is missing, Mags says only "Cold Kettle's
  late. The ice drift's thick this shift.", covering for Hessa by habit.
- *Revealed by:* the ring lit for this pilot (she hears it from Sefa), the player's
  memory 4 released in her office ([`player.md`](player.md)), and a later visit. Then
  she opens her watch book to that night and lets the player read the page: the flash,
  the bearing, "Cold Kettle: saw nothing." She says what she saw after, the child aboard
  Cold Kettle, and nothing more. She never tells the pilot they are that child; the
  page does, or doesn't.

**What she has started to wonder.** The ring never lit for anyone in all her years; it lit
for this pilot, who is about the right age. She says none of it until the page.

## Never says

- A name for the ring. She leaves it unnamed, even now.
- A bearing that is wrong, or a number she hasn't checked.
- Hessa's secret to anyone but the player.
- "You're that child", or anything that tells the player who they are or what to feel.
  She shows the page and stops.
- A guess about what the flash was.

## Kept true

- **"I'm Mags. I watch the field from here."** She did that night too. True.
- **"Your ring's on my oldest chart, unnamed."** The ring was on the chart before the
  flash, and she has kept it unnamed. True.
- **"Cold Kettle's late. The ice drift's thick this shift."** Read as habit, covering for
  Hessa. True to the line.
- **"Orrin can mend her engine for Hessa."**, **"Bram kept Cold Kettle for good. Hessa's
  grounded."**: she takes Hessa's side. True.
- **Mags hears of the ring from Sefa** (README, "The first rumour"): she learns it lit
  only from Sefa, and says nothing more then. True.
